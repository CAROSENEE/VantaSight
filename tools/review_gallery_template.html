Offline manual review: inspect only the recorded gameplay scene. Save all 12 planned frames so shortlist coverage is complete: draw one box around each visible in-world opponent, then select Y and save; select N when none is visible; select U when you cannot decide. Uncertain frames are excluded from metric calculations, but still need a saved review row. Do not label teammates, the streamer inset, first-person hands/weapons, HUD, or replay silhouettes. Click-drag on the image to draw boxes; zoom in when a distant enemy is small. Body-region points are optional and are only needed for pose/region evaluation: choose an opponent box, select a region, then click its approximate visual center. These are not anatomical or hitbox labels. Coordinates are saved in original source pixels. Nothing is sent over the network.

Shortlist sampling includes frames with model candidates and frames with zero model candidates across the video. Zero candidates do not mean an opponent is absent: review the recorded scene and choose Y, N, or U from what is visibly present.

Review frames are sampled from the exact video fingerprint shown above. This page does not show detector proposals. Export downloads a JSONL file only after you explicitly save reviewed frames.
